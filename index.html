Step 1

Which part of your work are you mapping?

Pick whatever applies — you can map more than one. This just helps orient the tool; you'll set the lens again for each individual step below, since a single workflow can touch more than one part of your role.

Step 2

Build your process map

Add one step at a time, in order, the way you'd describe it to a colleague. Don't worry about getting it perfect — a rough, honest description works better than a polished one. Answer the quick diagnostic under each step; that's what the tool uses to spot opportunities later.
